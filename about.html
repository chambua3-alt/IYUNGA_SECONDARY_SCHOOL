About Iyunga Secondary School

Iyunga Secondary School is committed to providing quality education and developing students academically, socially and morally.

About Our School

Iyunga Secondary School provides a supportive and conducive learning environment where students can acquire knowledge, develop important skills and build strong values for their future.

Our school encourages students to work hard, develop discipline and participate actively in both academic and extracurricular activities.

Our Mission and Vision

Our Mission

To provide quality education that equips students with knowledge, skills and values necessary for personal and academic development.

Our Vision

To become a centre of excellence for the provision of quality secondary education, science and technology

Our Core Values

Discipline

We encourage students to demonstrate discipline and responsibility in their daily activities.

Integrity

We promote honesty, respect and responsible behaviour among students and staff.

Excellence

We encourage students to work hard and strive for excellence in their education and activities.
